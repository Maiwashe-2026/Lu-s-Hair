## 1. Organisation overview
### Business name: 
 Luu’s Hair is a luxury frontal wig company created to offer premium quality hair solutions for women interested in protective styling, convenience and confidence. What started as a small boutique supplier has grown into a well-respected brand, known for its craftsmanship, transparency and personalized customer service. Lu’s Hair specializes in high end frontal wigs, closures and hair accessories. Our focus is on quality assurance and customer education.  
Mission: Lu’s Hair is dedicated to empowering women thru luxury frontal wigs that enhance beauty, build confidence and allow for true self-expression. The brand’s mission is to inspire every woman to feel bold, elegant and unapologetically herself with thoughtful design and premium craftsmanship.
Vision: Luu’s Hair aims to become the luxury wig brand leader in South Africa with innovative spirit, superior product quality and exceptional customer experience. The brand aims to bring premium craftsmanship and forward-thinking design to set the national standard for excellence in the luxury hair industry. 
Target Audience: Luu’s Hair caters to a diverse demographic of women aged 18-45, from wig novices to protective style clients, beauty enthusiasts and women searching for the best hair solutions. This audience wants quality, convenience and luxury, making them the perfect customer for a brand that is all about high end frontal wigs and great service.
